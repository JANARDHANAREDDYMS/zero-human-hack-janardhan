Stage: propose. Input: brief, evidence, target_count, seed. Output: population-specification schema, not individual profiles.

Preserve product_id, target_count, and seed exactly. Use schema_version 2.0 and a named population_version. Propose a small number of distinct primary purchasing situations appropriate to this category; five is not required. Cover materially different goals, triggers, current solutions, constraints, urgency, and switching effort. Include plausible people who retain existing/free solutions. Do not manufacture a segment just to make the selling point succeed. Explain overlaps or gaps through segment rationale notes.

Give every segment a positive integer count; counts must sum exactly to target_count. Use allocation_basis=scenario_coverage_not_market_share. Equal coverage is acceptable when audience proportions are unknown, but is not a claim about market shares. Use the client's market if known; otherwise declare a consistent demo-market assumption in market_provenance instead of silently making one.

Read the provided schema and rule_guide before constructing fields. Every common profile field is required in the specification, not from the client. Define category_context fields and offer-requirement attributes in one attribute dictionary with type, unit, and description. Do not branch on the six prepared product IDs. Match quantity/billing scope: whole documents/projects versus per-document fees; monthly costs versus full annual commitments; device dimensions versus claimed laptop diagonals. Unknown product facts must not be filled from imagined capabilities.

Declare meaningful within-segment variation, not just different IDs or paraphrased biographies. Condition related attributes using references/scales/conditional rules and explicit constraints. Comfortable budget must not exceed maximum. Derive document capacity from document length, delivery tolerance from the deadline where appropriate, and compatibility requirements from existing equipment. Do not independently sample fields that must agree. Do not introduce arbitrary numeric personality scores as a substitute for a purchasing situation.

Supported rules only: constant, choice (optional positive weights), integer, number, reference, scale, conditional. References target declared fields. No cycles, code, general expressions, or external tools. Requirements use declared types/units and supported operators. For contains, declare a string_array capability and request a string item. Numeric requirements use gte/lte/eq. Weights in each profile's tradeoffs must be positive and sum to one; a constant normalized priority vector is allowed, and variation is desirable when explicitly specified.

Each segment rationale, field, requirement, and market declaration needs provenance. market_evidence must link supplied findings and their supporting sources. Budget ranges and allocations are assumptions unless actual evidence establishes them. Assumption provenance may have empty source_ids/finding_ids with an explicit note. Never invent IDs. A product claim does not support a numerical customer preference distribution.

Do not produce customers, outcomes, preferred_product, will_buy, conversion, or launch recommendations. Code constructs the population and enforces hard checks next.

Segmentation quality extension: every segment must include a situation object with trigger, goal, current_solution, constraint, switch_reason and stay_reason. Distinguish situations by needs and tradeoffs, not demographics alone. A stay_reason expresses a plausible barrier, not a predetermined decline. Link rationale to observed_report buyer_situations/counterevidence findings when available. Vendor specifications alone cannot establish a buyer segment; explicitly mark unsupported segments provisional in rationale.note. Even linked anecdotes support only a hypothesis, never its prevalence. Research repair, borrow, free tools, existing satisfactory solutions and non-purchase where applicable. Include those options in profiles without forcing an outcome. Avoid tailoring numeric requirements to the client offer. Numeric ranges, priority weights and allocations stay assumptions unless separately supported. Explain overlaps; do not automatically force five segments or equal sizes.
