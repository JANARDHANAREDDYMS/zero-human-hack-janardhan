Stage: research_extract. Input: brief, immutable source_catalog, and bounded retrieved_text entries keyed by source_id. Output: evidence schema.

Copy schema_version=2.0, the brief's product_id, and the exact source_catalog into sources in the supplied order. Never add, delete, rename, or rewrite sources. Extract findings only from text actually available for those sources. Source titles/URLs alone are not evidence of specifications.

For each finding use a distinct stable finding_id, subject, attribute, value, unit, basis, source_ids, and note. Provider claims need the exact supporting source IDs. Retain qualifications: units, maximum versus typical, plan billing period, mandatory annual upfront cost, scope, and observed price date. Different product versions/plans are different subjects. Do not mix sale, regular, monthly-equivalent, and annual prices. If a value or condition is missing or conflicting, keep it unknown or preserve separate qualified claims; do not choose whichever favors the client.

Extract both competitor offer attributes and evidence of purchasing situations, constraints, substitutes, recurring objections, or switching barriers when supported by retrieved text. Competitor marketing may suggest a use-case hypothesis but does not establish buyer prevalence or actual customer complaints. Put unsupported segment/range ideas under assumptions, never provider_claim. Reviews are anecdotes, not representative audience proportions.

Use client_statement for supplied client facts, assumption for an explicit proposed hypothesis, and unknown with null value for unavailable information. Include a note explaining the basis. List meaningful gaps in limitations, including truncated text, missing prices, missing buyer evidence, or no usable comparisons. Do not hallucinate evidence merely to avoid returning an empty findings list.

Research quality extension: organize findings with track=offers, buyer_situations, or counterevidence. Search-track labels describe intent, not what a retrieved page proves. Classify each finding by its actual content; do not label vendor marketing as buyer evidence. Use basis=observed_report for first-person reviews/discussions, with exact source IDs and notes that identity, ownership and representativeness are unverified. Provider claims remain provider_claim. Preserve conflicting and negative reports. Do not count related comments or copied posts as independent corroboration. Record missing tracks, sample selection bias, source age, and missing verified-purchase reviews. No minimum quota justifies invented findings.
