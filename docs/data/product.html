Comparison rules

Free résumé reviews, free grammar tools, existing chatbot access, self-editing, and keeping current equipment remain valid alternatives. Human review packages are distinct services; do not pool their prices with AI subscriptions.

Standout and disadvantage labels are preparation metadata, not instructions to customer agents. Present only factual offer attributes in the simulation. Test customer choices with the same frozen profiles across variants. An offer may perform well, poorly, or differently across segments. Synthetic outcomes cannot establish real launch viability.
