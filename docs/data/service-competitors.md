# Service comparison research

Checked October 23, 2026. Initial official-source sample, not exhaustive market research. Provider feature claims are not independently tested performance. Proposed demo offers below require final agreement.

## Resume review alternatives

| Alternative | Observed price/basis | Relevant offering | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| TopResume review | Free | Resume critique and ATS analysis; advertised feedback in under 48 hours; not a full rewrite | https://topresume.com/resume-review |
| Rezi Pro | $29/month | Resume editor, score, keyword targeting, unlimited resumes; free tier also available | https://www.rezi.ai/pricing |
| Resume Worded Pro | $49/month, monthly plan | Complete report, line-by-line analysis, AI rewrites, resume and LinkedIn reviews | https://resumeworded.com/get-pro |
| Jobscan Premium | $49.95/month per provider comparison article | Resume/job matching and optimization; limited free version | https://www.jobscan.co/blog/jobscan-vs-teal/ |
| Existing AI chatbot or self-review | Depends on user's existing access and limits | DIY critique and rewriting; effort, file handling, and quality vary | Treat as a substitute; specific plans not verified in this research |

The median of these three selected paid monthly software offers ($29, $49, $49.95) is $49/month. This is NOT a market-wide median, a per-review median, or a justification for charging $49 for one review. Free review remains a real alternative. Jobscan's checkout page did not expose plan text; price above comes from its own article and should be reconfirmed before freezing competitor data.

Proposed baseline: one resume up to two pages, clarity/structure feedback, formatting checks, and suggested bullet edits. Earlier proposed standout: link each suggested rewrite to a requirement in one target job posting. Job-specific matching already exists among competitors, so this is positioning, not established uniqueness. Candidate refinement: every rewrite points both to the job requirement and the original resume evidence, flagging missing evidence rather than inventing experience. Superseded by the current proposed offer in competitor-baselines.json: $49/month, three reviews, 48-hour delivery. Those limits are deliberate scenario disadvantages, not medians.

## Proofreading alternatives

| Alternative | Observed price/basis | Relevant offering | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammarly Free | $0 | Spelling/grammar corrections and tone information | https://www.grammarly.com/plans |
| QuillBot Free | Free | Basic grammar corrections; other tools have usage limits | https://quillbot.com/premium |
| Scribbr AI proofreading | Page advertises $9.95 for one month, unlimited documents | DOCX tracked edits, grammar/spelling/clarity, turnaround at most ten minutes | https://www.scribbr.com/proofreading-services/ |
| Scribbr human editing | Page example $100 for 4,100 words; variable quote | Professional editing and tracked changes; service differs from automated tools | https://www.scribbr.com/proofreading-services/ |
| Existing AI chatbot or self-editing | Depends on existing access and limits | DIY editing; effort, formatting, and output quality vary | Specific chatbot plans not verified in this research |

Follow-up official sources confirm QuillBot Premium at $19.95 monthly (https://quillbot.com/blog/frequently-asked-questions/how-much-is-quillbot-premium/) and Grammarly Pro at $30 monthly (https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000090011-How-much-does-Grammarly-Pro-cost). Together with Scribbr AI at $9.95 for one month, the selected paid-tool median is $19.95/month. This is not a per-document price median. Free tools and human quotes remain separate alternatives. See competitor-baselines.json for current proposals.

Agreed candidate: $20 per document, up to 2,000 words, 48-hour delivery, grammar/spelling/punctuation/clarity edits. Proposed standout: preserve author tone and explain substantive changes. Tone features already exist, so unique superiority is not established. Candidate refinement: an edit ledger marking correction versus optional style change, with original/revised wording and reason. This refinement is proposed, not approved.

Retain the $20 price as a testable disadvantage rather than automatically lowering it. Customer-specific comparison includes total expected document volume, deadline, subscription commitment, existing tools, effort, confidence, and delivered format. No human proofreading is promised in our AI-based offer.

## Experiment

Freeze realistic needs before showing offers. Compare baseline without standout versus the same offer with the standout, retaining price and other attributes. Include real alternatives, free/DIY options, and no purchase. Observe choice changes by segment. Neither success nor rejection is predetermined. Synthetic choices support hypotheses under assumed profiles; they do not establish real-world launch viability or market conversion.
