## Implications for our demo (interpretation, not published judging criteria)
Show a service that completes a paid request and delivers useful, inspectable work. A customer-research service fits the business theme better than only a dashboard of invented conversion numbers. Show customer needs → observed simulation actions → a specific improvement hypothesis → targeted human feedback → revised comparison → delivered report.

The panel's product, engineering, AI, and commercial backgrounds suggest preparing concise answers about customer value, execution, model assumptions, costs, and why human input changes a decision. This is an inference; we do not know their scoring preferences or tomorrow's panel.

Use sponsor tools for distinct jobs: Whop order/payment, Exa evidence, Lovable app and intended runtime AI, Terac targeted human input. Verify capabilities and credit coverage before depending on them. A complete small journey is the primary demo; six prepared scenarios demonstrate breadth.
